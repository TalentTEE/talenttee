01

Problem & Solution

Solving the limits of the traditional hiring market with AI and blockchain

Problems with Traditional Hiring

  • Writing a resume takes significant time and effort
  • Real capabilities are not properly reflected in resumes
  • Manual matching creates inefficient hiring processes
  • Information asymmetry during salary negotiation
  • Opaque execution of employment contracts
  • Trust issues due to lack of payment guarantees

TalentTee's Solution

  • AI auto-generates resumes from real data (GitHub, Slack, etc.)
  • Objective skill analysis from code activity and communication patterns
  • Precise automatic matching via ANN + LLM reranking
  • AI agents represent both sides for fair negotiation
  • SHA-256 hash + NEAR blockchain guarantee agreement integrity
  • Smart contract escrow guarantees payment

EMPLOYER
  1. Create Job with AI Chat

  2. Publish Job Posting

Mutual Match Agreement
  1. AI Autonomous Negotiation
    Seeker Agent vs Employer Agent · encrypted multi-round · max 5
  2. Agreement & Approval
    Both parties review final agreement · SHA-256 hash · dual signing

    NEAR on-chain record · auto escrow settlement
