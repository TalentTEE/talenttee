Hiring is Broken

The traditional hiring pipeline is full of friction, bias, and broken trust.

Resume Theater

Resumes don't reflect real capabilities. Self-reported skills miss the truth. Candidates spend hours crafting narratives employers can't verify.

Negotiation Asymmetry

Information imbalance leads to unfair salary outcomes. Employers hold market data; candidates negotiate blind. The result is inefficiency and resentment.

Trust Deficit

No payment guarantees. Opaque processes. No verifiable agreements. When deals fall apart, there's no recourse and no record.

06

Under the Hood

Technical innovations that make TalentTee possible.

01

Hybrid AI Matching

ANN vector search via pgvector cosine similarity finds candidates fast. LLM reranking evaluates semantic fit precisely. A two-stage pipeline balancing speed and accuracy.

02

Encrypted Negotiation

Ed25519 key exchange derives HKDF session keys for AES-GCM encryption. Each negotiation round is end-to-end encrypted. Only participants can read the terms.

03

Agent Delegation

FunctionCall Access Keys let the server act on behalf of employers with capped allowance. Automated on-chain transactions with zero trust compromise.

04

Integrity Verification

SHA-256 hashes of final agreements stored on NEAR. Dual-party signing ensures mutual consent. Tamper-proof settlement records for both sides.
